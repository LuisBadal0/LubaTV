What's new in LubaTV v{{ memory.AppVersion }}

A home screen made for big windows

  • Wider cards with bigger logos, and Continue watching shows larger cards
  • Your favorite categories appear as rows on the home screen
  • Live channels no longer carry a LIVE label, and the source name only shows when you have more than one

Favorite categories in the sidebar

  • Open a category and choose Add to sidebar, or right-click a category tile
  • Favorite categories stay after a source refresh

A channel list inside categories

  • Categories show their channels as a list, with a preview pane beside it
  • The pane shows the TV guide for Xtream channels, a Watch button and a favorite button

Search by country

  • Type a country code in capitals, such as PT or US, to see that country's categories and channels
  • Keep typing to search within that country. Backspace or the × removes the country
  • Typed in lowercase, a code searches names as before, and LubaTV offers the country as a suggestion

Better names for downloads and recordings

  • Files from addresses without an extension, such as http://provider.com/live/123, are saved as .mp4 instead of getting a broken name
  • Addresses with something after the file name, such as 42.mkv?token=…, keep their real extension

Much smaller updates

  • Updates now download about 10 MB instead of about 100 MB: they no longer include mpv, ffmpeg and yt-dlp
  • LubaTV downloads those only when a new version of one of them is out, and only the ones that changed
  • Each one is checked against its published checksum before it's used

A cleaner window

  • Minimize, maximize and close now sit in the window's top-right corner instead of a separate title bar, and close turns red when you point at it
  • The scrollbar starts below those buttons instead of running beside them
  • Drag the header or the logo to move the window, and double-click it to maximize

Faster channel switching

  • Picking another channel now switches the open player window instead of closing it and opening a new one
  • The window keeps its size, position, fullscreen and volume, and comes back to the front
  • Closing the player, or LubaTV, still closes it completely

Clearer playback errors

  • When a channel won't play, LubaTV says why, such as a refused login, an expired subscription or a server that's down, and what to try
  • A dead live channel gives up after about 15 seconds instead of a minute or more
