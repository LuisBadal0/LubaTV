Privacy Policy

LubaTV does not collect, store or share any personal information.

The app has no accounts, analytics or telemetry. Your sources, favorites and settings are
stored only on your device.

LubaTV connects to the IPTV sources you add and to the stream and logo addresses they list.
When you re-stream a channel, it also asks ipify (api.ipify.org) for your public IP address
to build the share link.
